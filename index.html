A Note:

Namaste, In this fast world, many find it difficult to listen to Vedanta by attending classes. In an effort to make it simple for everyone and also as small snippets so that people can just listen for 5 minutes even if there is no time these snippets have been recorded. For me, these snippets are the temple iam building for the divine with the deity being krishna. Great mahans have walked this earth and have written bhasyams. I have tried my best to explain each sloka to my capacity. This would have not been possible without the grace and teachings of my gurus (Shri Goda Venkateshwara Sastrigal and Shri Advaita Jyothiraditya Shrouthi) I dedicate this at the holy feet of my gurus. Thank you!
