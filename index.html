A Note:

Namaste,

In this fast world, many find it difficult to listen to Vedanta by attending classes.

These snippets were thought of and recorded so that everyone can listen for few minutes everyday.

This is currently being done for bhagavad gita in english and tamil. Chapter 6 is ongoing right now. I have tried to explain each sloka to the best of my capacity.

The other recordings are class recordings of other advaita prakarana granthas like tattvabodha, atmabodha and strotra granthas like Soundarya lahari.

Ongoing class recordings of Lalitha Sahasranamam, Vivekachoodamani, Mooka Panchasathi , Narayaneeyam are also uploaded for easy reference.

This would have not been possible without the grace and teachings of my gurus (Shri Goda Venkateshwara Sastrigal and Shri Advaita Jyothiraditya Shrouthi) I dedicate this at the holy feet of my gurus. Thank you!
